# Iva — support

Say it, and Iva files it. Iva listens to what you say, works out which of your tables it belongs in, fills in the columns, and saves the row — all on your iPhone or iPad.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[noujan72@gmail.com](mailto:noujan72@gmail.com)** or [open an issue](https://github.com/noujan/iva-legal/issues). We usually reply within a few days.

### Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. Iva has no accounts, no server and no subscription.

**Where is my data?** On your device only, in the app's own storage. To move it, use Settings → Export to make a CSV per table or a full backup file.

**Why does Iva put a note in the wrong table?** Iva's sorting can sometimes guess wrong. The review screen shows the row before it is saved so you can fix or move it.

**The microphone doesn't work.** Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition, and make sure Iva is allowed. Speech recognition needs a real device; it is not available in the Simulator.

**Which devices are supported?** iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later.
